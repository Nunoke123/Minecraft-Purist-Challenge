Minecraft Hardcore: The Purist Challenge

> Stripping away the meta to enforce the raw survival experience.

The following rules are made to make the intended Minecraft Hardcore Experience by taking away all forms of “cheating” and meta-gaming while also adding some extra challenges.

General Gameplay Rules

  1. No Commands or Save Editing: Strictly no cheating death, world rollbacks, file manipulation, or console command usage.
  2. No Mods or Datapacks: Pure vanilla progression only. Quality performance-optimization mods (such as Sodium or Optifine) are the only exceptions.
  3. No Visual Exploits: Texture packs or client modifications providing X-ray vision, Fullbright, or clear water are strictly forbidden.
  4. No Debug Features: The use of the F3 debug screen, pie charts, chunk borders (F3+G), and hitboxes (F3+B) is completely barred.
  5. No Subtitles: Spatial awareness must rely purely on directional audio; visual threat sound indicators are disabled.
  6. No Beds: You may not sleep to skip the night or suppress Phantom spawns. Nighttime must be braved or survived in hiding.
  7. No Nether Roof: All navigation, traversal, and building within the Nether dimension must occur safely beneath the bedrock ceiling.
  8. No Villagers or Automation: Village trading loops are prohibited. All automatic resource farms are banned; every item must be hand-mined, hand-harvested, or manually hunted.
  9. No Bug Exploitation: Item duplication, breaking bedrock, glitching through blocks,...
  10. No External Assistance: No set seeds, coordinate calculators, nether portal calculators, or seed-cracking programs allowed.
  11. No Hardware Alterations: Forcing monitor gamma settings or brightness presets to artificially see in deep caves or nighttime environments is prohibited. Torches are mandatory.

Extra Rules (Dismissible)

  1. No rockets for flight: Get to a High Place and jump, it's not that hard.
  2. Ultra HardCore: Turn off natural regeneration.
[!] Accessibility Exception: Any structural rules regarding subtitles, brightness levels, or audio indicators may be fully dismissed for visually or auditorily impaired players.

Multiplayer Scaling Rules

While an alliance brings strength in numbers, the world fractures to balance the scales. As more players enter the world, severe limitations trigger dynamically based on player count:

  • 1 Player: Standard core rules apply. Full armor tiers, defensive shields, golden healing apples, and natural food regeneration remain enabled.
  • 2 Players: The survival dynamic shifts. Both active players must forfeit all armor entirely.
  • 3 Players: Defense strips down further. All active players must forfeit all armor AND all shields.
  • 4 Players: All players must forfeit armor and shields. Additionally, Natural Regeneration is completely disabled (UHC Mode).

Roster & Bench Mechanics

  • A hard maximum of 4 active players may occupy the world concurrently.
  • You must play with a fixed core group. When an active player dies, they are permanently eliminated from the world. A benched player may step in to take their slot.
  • Inactivity Swap: To accommodate real-world scheduling, a player can be manually swapped out for a benched player. To maintain the brutal stakes, that swapped-out player is legally registered as "deceased" and can never rejoin this world file. Make sure to get permission from the player before they are kicked!
  • Dead players may only return when a new world is created.

End Goals

You may notice some of these don't seem too challenging, and that's because they aren't. Make your own path, as long as you have fun while doing it!

  1. Defeat the Ender Dragon
  2. Summon and defeat the Wither
  3. Construct and activate a full-power Beacon
  4. Fill a complete Double Chest with Poisonous Potatoes
  5. Tame a Wolf, equip it with some Stylish Wolf Armor and a Cool Name
  6. Tame a Horse, equip it with high-tier Netherite Horse Armor, and bestow a Legendary Name
  7. Build a house out of dirt and a house out of diamond blocks right next to each other
  8. Build a classic Aether Portal and a Twilight Forest portal side-by-side
  9. Construct a Herobrine Spawner with Gold blocks, Netherrack, Redstone Torches, and Fire
  10. Craft a set of Leather Armor dyed Green, with an Emerald Armor Trim
  11. Construct an epic Throne crafted out of your most valuable blocks
  12. Forge a max-enchanted Golden Helmet and name it "Crown"
